🎯 The Expertise Inflation Index (EII)

📖 Let's Define Some Things

🧠 EXPERTISE

"The skill or knowledge in a particular area that makes someone an expert"

Or at least what we think we have...

📈 INFLATION

"A general increase in prices and fall in purchasing value"

But applied to confidence and jargon!

📊 INDEX

"A system used to measure changes in a specified group"

Like the S&P 500, but for AI hot air!

🔗 Put them together and you get...

🎯 EXPERTISE INFLATION INDEX

"A system to measure the inflation of confidence, jargon, and authority claims in AI-related content"

🎯 The EII Scoring System

💪 Confidence Inflation

How certain are you about uncertain things?

1 = Humble → 10 = "I am the AI Oracle"

🤖 Jargon Density

How many buzzwords per paragraph?

1 = Plain English → 10 = "Synergistic paradigm shifts"

🏆 Self-Reference/Authority

How often do you mention your credentials?

1 = Objective → 10 = "As a thought leader..."

✨ Originality Claims

How revolutionary is your "breakthrough"?

1 = Incremental → 10 = "Never been done before!"

😂 Humor/Self-Awareness

Do you know you're being ridiculous?

1 = Deadly serious → 10 = "This is all nonsense"

🕷️ Firecrawl Configuration

from firecrawl import FirecrawlApp

app = FirecrawlApp(api_key=os.getenv('FIRECRAWL_API_KEY'))

def scrape_article(url):
    response = app.scrape_url(url, params={
        'formats': ['markdown'],
        'only_main_content': True,
        'exclude_tags': ['nav', 'footer', 'ads']
    })
    return response['data']['markdown']
